How it works

Three simple steps to get your IT issue resolved.

1

Submit a ticket

Log in and describe the IT issue you're experiencing. Attach a screenshot if it helps.

2

Get assigned

The Helpdesk Manager reviews the ticket and assigns it to the right technician.

3

Track & resolve

The technician works on the issue and updates the status until it's resolved.

Why use the portal?

Everything you need to get help, no more chasing emails or phone calls. Every issue gets a ticket number you can reference any time. See exactly where your ticket is Open, Assigned, In Progress, or Resolved. A picture is worth a thousand words, so attach a screenshot to your ticket. And role-based access means staff, technicians, and managers each see exactly what they need.

About the portal

This IT Helpdesk serves the staff of the O.R. Tambo District Municipality. It provides a single, transparent channel for reporting and resolving technology-related issues across all departments, replacing scattered emails and phone calls with a clear, trackable workflow.
